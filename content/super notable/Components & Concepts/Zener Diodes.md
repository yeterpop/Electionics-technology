Used in modern components as a voltage regulator. When a device gets a certain amount of voltage, the Zenner diodes protects it by breaking a barrier within the diode allowing a specified amount of voltage through it.

Zenner diodes are used in reverse bias because it relies on the barrier breaking for it to conduct. its Intended to take most of the current from load component to prevent over loading of device.  

Used with modern chips/components on circuit boards, as the voltage varies for those components depending on load.
