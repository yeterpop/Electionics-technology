#### Explaining the difference between Resistor and capacitor voltage

The capacitor voltage is different, because the negative side of the capacitor is directly hooked up to ground, where as the load resistor is in parallel with the Zener diode. The 270 ohm resistor doesn't cause a huge drop in voltage, but the Zener diode regulates anything above 12V. With out the Zener diode, the voltage would be 19V.
