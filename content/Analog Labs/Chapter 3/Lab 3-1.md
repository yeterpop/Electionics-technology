### Voltage values explained
ㅤ
**Why is there less voltage?**

There is about half the voltage in the DC part of the circuit, because half of the wave is being converted instead of the full wave.
ㅤ
ㅤ
ㅤ

**What happens when you flip the PN junction?** 

When flipping the PN junction the other way, the AC voltage stays the same, but the DC voltage becomes negative as a result. Now the output is the other half of the wave.
ㅤ
