

## Damage
#### Insulation Damage **5.1.1**

- Shall not have cuts or breaks, cracks or splits
- No melting insulation within the strands
- Have it's thickness cut by 20%
- Have uneven pieces of insulation (or tails greater than 50% of the insulation diameter)
- Burns or chars

#### Strand Damage **5.1.2**

- Don't have a diameter of strand separated from other strands (bird caging)

# Tinning Stranded Wires

### When to tin stranded wires [N1D2D3]
- soldering to terminals
- wires will be formed into splices (other than mesh)
- Optional when heat shrinkable solder devices are used

### When not to stranded tin wires [D1D2D3]
- When used in a crimp [[Terminations]] 
- When used in threaded fasteners
- When used in forming mesh splices

### Where to tin (coverage)
- Don't tin a part of a wire that is supposed to be flexible. [D1D2D3]
- Solder shall be inbetween the strands [N1D2D3]
- There shall not be voids pinholes or dry (nonwetting area) up to 5% in the wire [A1P2D3]
